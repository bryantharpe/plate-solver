# Plan: specs for "where do I want to go, and how do I get there" (feat-12 → feat-15)

## Context

The finder console (feat-11, merged) answers *what am I looking at*: it plate-solves one frame and charts it. The owner wants the next part:
- pick a target and learn whether it can be pointed at;
- get push-to directions, or have the 16-inch Dob driven there, without star alignment;
- keep it there, with tracking pushed toward imaging;
- in the console's field-atlas design;
- with all hardware behind swappable adapters.

The `/grill-with-docs` session settled every decision below and already wrote:
- `GLOSSARY.md`, the single glossary (`openspec/project.md` §6 now points to it);
- `docs/adr/0001-console-owns-pointing-scope-node-drives-axes.md`;
- the PRD rename of "tracking mode" to "hinted solving".

It also installed Node 22 and OpenSpec 1.14.3 in WSL, and the owner approved this plan on 2026-10-21.

**Carrying out this plan writes specs and docs only, no product code.** The build starts after the owner has validated the specs.

**Read first:** `GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/adr/0001-console-owns-pointing-scope-node-drives-axes.md`, and the research notes the decisions rest on:
- `docs/research/2026-10-21-synscan-mount-control.md`

## Settled decisions

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Order | 1. feat-12 targets and push-to (no hardware). 2. feat-13 live finder. 3. feat-14 GoTo. 4. feat-15 tracking. SkySafari, AI and the mobile app are out of scope. |
| Targets | OpenNGC only (NGC, IC and addendum), searchable by Messier number, NGC/IC id and common name, bundled for offline use. Typed RA/Dec, named stars, and the Moon and planets are deferred. |
| Pointable | Below 0° the target is *below the horizon*; below the *minimum altitude* (one number, default 15°) it is *too low*. The *Sun guard*: nothing within 15° of the Sun, no motorised moves while the Sun is up, always on, no override. Targets that are down get rise, transit and set times. |
| UI | The existing console plus a Setup page, in DESIGN.md's field-atlas language. The console gets a target search sheet; a guidance block ("turn clockwise 34° · tilt up 12°"; Go, Stop and Track later); and the target on the chart, or an edge arrow when it is off the frame. Setup holds the site, minimum altitude, scope node, camera, aim point, model fit and imaging camera. The UI stays light. |
| Session | The server holds one observing session shared by every device: site typed once, minimum altitude, target, and so on. Time comes from the console's clock. |
| Hardware | A *scope node* (Pi 3B on the telescope) hosts the Arducam CSI finder camera (model unknown) and a USB cable straight to the mount's motor controller. It finds stars and carries out axis commands. The console (Surface/WSL) solves, models, plans and serves the page. Ports and adapters per ADR-0001. All three devices use the house Wi-Fi (Starlink). |
| Alignment | No star alignment. Every solve while the mount is connected is a *sync*. IA, IE and the base tilt come from two syncs at least 90° apart in azimuth (one tap, or as you observe). CA, NPAE, TF and the finder mounting come from about 20 syncs and are kept between nights. |
| Aim point | Set on Setup by tapping a star centred in the eyepiece, and kept until the camera moves. Until then, the frame centre is used. |
| GoTo | Closed loop to 1′: slew at top speed, final approach from a fixed direction, at most 5 corrections. Stop or a lost link halts the mount. Cable wrap is unwound past one turn. |
| Tracking | Pointing-model rates plus PI correction from solves every 1–5 s. Within 2° of the zenith the target passes through. Field rotation and the longest exposure are reported for the imaging camera. |
| Simulator | A full simulated scope node: an off-level mount with backlash, an aim-point offset, and star fields from the catalog. Runs as `finder --simulate` and drives the CI end-to-end tests. |
| Names | finder console, finder camera, finder scope, scope node. *Turn* is clockwise or anticlockwise as seen from above; *tilt* is up or down. |

## Conventions for every change

**OpenSpec 1.14.3** (checked against its validator):
- **Statement:** at most 500 characters (aim for about 300), with SHALL/MUST in the first sentence and glossary terms exactly.
- **References:** one physical line right after the statement, e.g. `**Refs**: Meeus ex. 15.a; ADR-0001`. OpenSpec doesn't count `**Label**:` lines toward the 500. No code paths or HTML comments in requirements; archive drops the comments.
- **Scenarios:** WHEN/THEN, each naming its oracle and an explicit tolerance.
- **New capability:** its delta opens with `## Purpose` (at least 50 characters) and only ADDs.
- **Later changes only ADD** to capabilities introduced earlier in this set. MODIFIED validates, but archive refuses it until the base is archived; this was tested on a throwaway copy. So write statements open-ended ("at least", never "only").
- **MODIFIED** requirements copy the whole requirement and keep every scenario name.
- **Why** section: 50–1000 characters. **tasks.md**: `## N.` groups with `- [ ] N.M` items.

**The oracle rule.** No spec may require editing an existing test.
- Prefer designs that keep existing tests valid.
- Where a decided change supersedes tested behaviour, name it as a MODIFIED/REMOVED delta, in Impact and in tasks, for the owner's explicit approval.
- Shapes existing tests pin down:
  - the signatures of `AppState::new`/`shared`, `router` and `similar()`;
  - the fields of `Observer` and `Frame` (tests build them as struct literals);
  - the five legend chips;
  - `useSite` and SiteDialog's props;
  - NextTargets' `getSimilar` URL;
  - the existing fixtures (new report fields are optional);
  - App.test's fetch mock;
  - `alt_az` stays geometric (the Meeus 13.b test).
- `crates/math-core` is untouched (it's the parity path).

**CI realities** (ci.yml, deny.toml):
- **Coverage:** clippy, coverage and docs build with `--all-features` on x86_64, so feature gates don't hide hardware code.
  - Keep the hardware shells (opening the serial port, spawning rpicam) tiny.
  - Test everything else through the ports, a byte-level protocol emulator, saved fixtures and the simulator.
  - GoTo, sync and tracking must be proven by Rust integration tests in virtual time, because the 80% floor counts Rust only.
- **Clocks:** the clock is injectable everywhere. The virtual clock is allowed only in tests and `--simulate`; anywhere else it would be a way around the Sun guard.
- **Build gates:**
  - generated gRPC code gets cargo-machete ignores and a clippy allow, as in grpc-service;
  - no `[...]` in proto comments (rustdoc runs with `-D warnings`);
  - dependencies must build on Rust 1.96 and pass cargo-deny (serialport is MPL-2.0, which is allowed; use `default-features = false` to avoid libudev);
  - test tokens must have low entropy (secret scan);
  - no new npm packages (the notices test pins them).
- **Playwright:**
  - new servers or projects are named in the proposal;
  - no long-lived request while no scope node is connected, because the offline e2e test waits for `networkidle`.

**UI**:
- Follow DESIGN.md: field atlas, square ruled boxes, red-only night mode. Every new view goes into the night pixel check.
- Each change with UI ends with an Impeccable pass (shape → craft → finish review) and a DESIGN.md update.
- feat-12 adds a surface brief for the Setup page under `.impeccable/surfaces/`.

**Reuse**:
- `crates/finder/src/sky.rs`: `alt_az`, `precess_from_j2000`, `gmst_deg`, `julian_date`, and `Frame::project`, `direction_deg` and `north_at`.

## feat-12-targets-and-push-to

**Capabilities:** new `observing-session`, `target-catalog`, `sky-ephemeris` and `push-to`; `finder-console` gets MODIFIED and ADDED requirements. Format below: requirement — behaviour → oracle.

**`observing-session`**
- **One session for every device** — `GET`/`PUT /api/session` covers site, minimum altitude, target, latest pointing and console time → what client A writes, client B reads; a fresh server has no site and no target, and a minimum of 15°.
- **Checked edits** — site within ±90°/±180°, minimum altitude 0–80°, target by catalog id; anything else gets 400 or 404 with a JSON error → latitude 95 gives 400; `NGC 99999` gives 404.
- **Kept across restarts** — the binary keeps the session in a state file (by default under `~/.local/state/finder/`, moved with `--state`), written atomically. The library's default is in-memory, so tests write nothing.
- **Same-origin writes** — every write route needs JSON and an `Origin` that matches or is absent, so a page on another site can't drive the console → a foreign Origin gives 403.
- **Latest pointing** — each solved frame becomes the pointing: J2000 at the aim point (the frame centre until it is calibrated), alt/az, and the solve time → orion2 under a fixed clock.

**`target-catalog`**
- **All of OpenNGC, offline** — every NGC, IC and addendum object except `NonEx`, with each `Dup` resolved, agreeing with `messier.csv` → the object count; all 110 Messier objects within 1″ (M102 is NGC 5866, M76 is NGC 650, M45 is Mel 22).
- **Search** — `GET /api/targets?q=` matches Messier and NGC/IC ids (any case, optional space, leading zeros) and common-name words; exact ids first, then by magnitude → `m31`, `ngc0224` and `andromeda` each give NGC 224 first; `qqq` gives an empty list.

**`sky-ephemeris`** (lives in `finder`, not math-core)
- **The Sun** (Meeus ch. 25) → example 25.a within 0.01°.
- **Rise, transit and set** of any given altitude (ch. 15), or a statement that it never crosses → example 15.a within ±1 min; circumpolar and never-rises cases.

**`push-to`**
- **Pointable** — classed by precedence as Sun guard, below the horizon, too low, or pointable, with rise/transit/set times for targets that are down → at 40° N 75° W, 2026-01-16 02:00 UT: M42 pointable, M8 below the horizon, M75 Sun guard; M42 too low with a 50° minimum.
- **Turn and tilt** — for pointable targets only: Δaz wrapped to ±180° (clockwise means azimuth growing) and Δalt → from 350°/30° to 24°/42° is "turn clockwise 34° · tilt up 12°".
- **A still telescope stays put** — the solved pointing's alt/az holds while the target's follows the clock.
- **Placed on the frame** — the target is placed the way objects are, or given as its direction from the frame centre → for orion2, M78's mark equals its object mark.

**`finder-console`**
- MODIFIED **The observing site** — edits the session's site.
  - Typed in, plus the existing "Use this device's location" button, which stays along with its tests. "Typed only" means no new GPS work.
  - It adopts, once, a site this browser stored before.
- MODIFIED **Objects like this one** — "up" means at or above the minimum altitude, via a new `similar_above()` beside `similar()`.
- MODIFIED **What is in the field, and what to look at next** — "below 15°" shows the minimum altitude instead.
- MODIFIED **Where it points comes first** — with a target set, the guidance block comes first and the roster moves below the chart. That is the default; the design pass confirms it. With no target, today's first screen is unchanged.
- ADDED:
  - **Choosing a target** (the search sheet);
  - **The guidance block** (polls every 5 s or less);
  - **The target on the chart** (not a legend layer; included in the night pixel check);
  - **Setup page** at `/setup` (site and minimum altitude).

**Design defaults:**
- `finder::session` behind an injectable clock.
- A `data/openngc.csv` extract: about 1 MB, CC BY-SA 4.0, from a pinned commit, documented in `data/README.md`, with a THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES row.
- The chart keeps drawing Messier objects only; adding NGC/IC objects would break the orion2 and Virgo tests.

**Tasks:**
1. Session and API.
2. OpenNGC extract and search.
3. Ephemeris.
4. Push-to.
5. Page: session hook, sheet, guidance block, chart mark, Setup.
6. Tests: API, Vitest, and a second Playwright server for the tests that write to the session.
7. Impeccable pass and DESIGN.md.

## feat-13-live-finder

**Capabilities:** new `scope-link`, `scope-node`, `simulator` and `live-finder`; ADDED requirements to `observing-session` and `finder-console`.

**`scope-link`**
- **Paired link** — gRPC `scope_node.v1` with a token; calls without it get UNAUTHENTICATED.
- **Star frames**, each with:
  - an id;
  - exposure start and end on the node's clock;
  - frame size, exposure, gain and detection settings;
  - up to 200 centroids, brightest first;
  - a preview JPEG of at most 640 px, at most every 5 s or on request.
- **Clock mapping** — a heartbeat at least every 250 ms; the NTP-style offset of the least-delay exchange → a worked t0–t3 example gives +0.050 s.
- **Settings from the console** — `DetectParams::cedar()` unless Setup overrides it.

**`scope-node`**
- **Camera port** — reports sensor, modes and exposure/gain ranges; yields 8-bit gray frames with exposure times.
- **Arducam via an rpicam-apps subprocess** — takes the Y plane of `rpicam-vid` YUV420, and reads the model from `rpicam-hello --list-cameras`, all tested with fixtures.
- **Same centroids** as the console's detection for the same frame.
- **Auto-exposure** — holds 25–40 stars, aiming at 30; manual settings hold → on the simulator, from 5 stars to in range within 6 frames.
- **Configured service** — a config file; `--check-config` prints it with the token masked.

**`simulator`** (camera half)
- **The same surface as the node** — star fields from the pattern database's stars, centroid noise, an aim-point offset and a virtual clock → a frame at alt 50°, az 120° solves within 30″.
- **Pushed by hand** — a simulator-only control moves the simulated telescope.
- **`finder --simulate`** — with `--sim-start`; it never touches a real node.

**`live-finder`**
- **Live solving** — takes the newest frame and drops stale ones; the solved FOV becomes the next estimate; the pointing is at the mapped mid-exposure time → at 2 frames/s for 10 s, every solve is within 30″.
- **Live chart** — previews pass through the night filter. Server-sent events run only while a node is connected; with no node the page behaves as it does today.
- **Aim point** — a tap snaps to the nearest centroid within 10 px and is kept until reset → the simulator's (700, 420) is recovered within 0.5 px.
- **Fresh pointing** — guidance uses only a pointing at most 5 s old.

**Also ADDED:**
- `observing-session`: node address and token (write-only), camera settings, aim point.
- `finder-console`: Setup gets a scope node and camera section: connection, model, star count, exposure, gain, σ, and the solved FOV against the database's range, with a warning.

**Design defaults:**
- **New crates:**
  - `scope-link`: its proto lives in the crate, because this internal link isn't part of the public `proto/` API;
  - `scope-node`: a library and binary, depending on star-detection only (not plate-solver or pattern-database);
  - `simulator`.
- **Pi build:** the node is built in WSL as static `aarch64-unknown-linux-musl`, because Pi OS's glibc is older; the systemd unit is documented.
- **Camera access:** rpicam-apps rather than libcamera bindings, since bindings need headers and libclang, which the `--all-features` CI lacks.

**Risks:**
- The exact Arducam model, and whether it works on a Pi 3B.
- **The finder camera's field of view**, which is probably outside the bundled 10–30° database. Measure it at bring-up and build a matching database with `database-generation`.
- 2.4 GHz-only Wi-Fi; the camera ISP's tone curve; NTP clock steps on the Pi.

**Tasks:**
1. Bring-up on the Pi 3B: camera works, FOV, detection time per frame, Wi-Fi throughput.
2. Link.
3. Node and service docs.
4. Simulator camera half and `--simulate`.
5. Live loop.
6. Page and aim-point calibration.
7. Tests: Rust end-to-end over an in-process link in virtual time, plus a Playwright `--simulate` project.
8. Manual on-sky checklist.

## feat-14-goto

**Capabilities:** new `pointing-model` and `goto`; ADDED requirements to `scope-node`, `scope-link`, `simulator`, `sky-ephemeris`, `observing-session` and `finder-console`.

**`scope-node`**
- **Mount port** — two axes, each with:
  - position and capabilities;
  - go to a position;
  - move at a signed rate;
  - stop.
- **Sky-Watcher adapter**, speaking the ':' protocol:
  - 24-bit little-endian hex, with the 0x800000 offset on positions;
  - initialise with `:F`; read `:e`, `:a`, `:b`, `:g`;
  - probe 9600 baud, then 115200;
  - auxiliary encoders off via `:W`;
  - stop an axis before reversing it.

  Oracles against the emulator: position 0 is `000080`, +1 is `010080`, −1 is `FFFF7F`; a goto to +0x123 on axis 1 sends `:S1230180\r`; a `!2` reply is handled.
- **Stop wins, plus a watchdog** — both axes stop 1 s after the last heartbeat → 1.0 ± 0.1 s in virtual time.
- **Axis positions on frames**, at the start and end of each exposure.

**Other ADDED requirements:**
- **`scope-link`:** mount calls (axis state, go to, move at rate, stop all).
- **`simulator`** (mount half): a base 1° off level, zero-point offsets, 0.3° of backlash, a top rate of 3.4°/s with acceleration, and the true eyepiece direction exposed to tests.
- **`sky-ephemeris` — Apparent altitude:** Saemundsson's formula adds refraction and Bennett's removes it; `alt_az` stays geometric → R(0°) = 34.5′ ± 0.1′; R(45°) = 1.01′.

**`pointing-model`**
- **Every solve is a sync** — when the axes are still or at tracking rates; the apparent alt/az at the aim point at mid-exposure, with interpolated axis positions; thinned to one sync per 2° per 2 min.
- **Zero points and tilt** — IA and IE from one sync; AN and AW from two syncs at least 90° apart.
  - Synthetic terms are recovered within 1″.
  - One sync on a base 1° off level leaves 2.00° ± 0.01° of error at the opposite azimuth.
- **Slips** — a sync more than 0.5° off the model opens a new zero-point epoch → after a 3° hand push, the next GoTo still ends within 1′.
- **Fixed terms** — CA, NPAE, TF and the finder mounting, fitted from at least 20 syncs spanning 180° of azimuth and 30° of altitude, then persisted → 24 synthetic syncs fit each term within 1″; with 19, none is fitted.
- **One-tap second sync** — slews to a pointable, Sun-safe spot at least 90° away and solves there.

**`goto`**
- **Plan** — goes through refraction and the model at the expected arrival time, keeping azimuth within one turn of where the session began → at +350°, a target at azimuth 20° is reached by turning anticlockwise 330°.
- **Sun guard on motion** — no moves while the Sun is up; no slew path within 15° of the Sun, re-planned by axis order.
- **Closed loop to 1′** — at most 5 corrections, or a reported failure → on the simulator, five targets each end with the true eyepiece within 1′.
- **Halts** — Stop, a lost link or the Sun guard leave the axes stopped, with the reason reported.
- **Pointable only** — `POST /api/goto` answers 409 for a target that isn't pointable.

**Also ADDED:**
- `observing-session`: zero points, tilt and syncs last one night; the fixed terms persist.
- `finder-console`: GoTo and Stop in the guidance block, with progress; Setup gets a mount and model section ("tilt 0.8° toward 130°", RMS, sync count).

**Design defaults:**
- `serialport` with `default-features = false`, or a minimal termios open.
- If `:S` fails, fall back to `:H`/`:M`.
- Freedom Find is off while the console drives.
- **Stop needs no token**, so anyone can halt the telescope; moves do need it.
- Aim-point accuracy depends on centring the star by eye, so calibrate with a high-power eyepiece.
- If feat-14 grows too big for one review, split it into 14a (mount link) and 14b (pointing model and GoTo).

**Tasks:**
1. Codec, adapter and emulator.
2. Watchdog and Stop.
3. Link.
4. Simulator mount.
5. Refraction.
6. Pointing model.
7. GoTo.
8. Page and Setup.
9. Virtual-time Rust end-to-end tests, plus a Playwright GoTo flow.
10. Manual on-sky checks.

## feat-15-tracking

**Capabilities:** new `tracking`; ADDED requirements to `sky-ephemeris`, `simulator`, `observing-session` and `finder-console`.

**`sky-ephemeris`**
- **Alt-az rates** → at φ 40°, δ 20°: H 0 gives dh/dt 0 and dA/dt 41.33″/s; H −30° gives +10.07″/s and 18.45″/s.
- **Field rotation and exposure** → at transit, 33.69°/h; a 23.5 × 15.6 mm sensor with 3.76 µm pixels allows 1.63 s.

**`tracking`**
- **Feed-forward** — pointing-model rates, updated at least once a second.
- **Closed by the finder** — solves every 1–5 s feed PI control with a 10″ deadband → with a 0.5% rate error, feed-forward alone drifts more than 1′ in 10 min; with PI the RMS stays at or under 20″.
- **Zenith keyhole** — pass, turn 180°, re-solve, resume → for δ = φ − 1°, back within 1′.
- **Ends safely** — on Stop, a lost link, sunrise, or the target falling below the minimum altitude, and says why.

**Also ADDED:**
- `simulator`: a rate-error setting.
- `observing-session` and `finder-console`: a Track toggle with a readout of error, field rotation and longest exposure; Setup gets the imaging camera's sensor size (mm) and pixel pitch (µm).

**Design:**
- Long simulated runs feed the console the simulator's true pointing plus noise, rather than instrumented solves, so the coverage job stays fast.
- The altitude axis reverses at transit, which relies on feat-14's stop-before-reverse.

**Tasks:**
1. Rates and field rotation.
2. Tracking loop.
3. Zenith keyhole.
4. Page and Setup.
5. Virtual-time tests.
6. Manual on-sky checks.
