## Reading the numbers

- All three solvers solve all 8 frames and agree on the sky position to
  within ~19 arcseconds worst-case — for scale, cedar-solve itself deviates
  from tetra3 by up to 13.6″ on the same frames.
- The Rust solver's full image→solution wall time is ~1.9× faster than both
  Python implementations on this corpus (median 8.4 ms vs ~16 ms), including
  star detection, on a database of a million patterns.
- Known gap: the Rust solver's reported RMSE over matched stars runs higher
  (median 24″ vs ~6″). Boresight accuracy is unaffected (see the agreement
  table); the difference comes from the refinement stage's match-set
  composition and is tracked as an open item.
